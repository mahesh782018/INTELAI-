About INTELAI Solutions Private Limited

INTELAI Solutions Private Limited is an IT training and software development company dedicated to providing industry-oriented training and career opportunities for students and working professionals.

Established in 2025, the organization focuses on practical learning, real-time projects, internships, certification programs, and placement assistance to prepare learners for successful careers in the IT industry.
